KEMOSH

Start in a white void. Walk around your room and it builds itself back up around you out of blocks — then walk through it, and see it from any side.

No AR on this phone? Stand in one spot and turn instead:

  1. You start in white. Nothing is known yet, so there is nothing to draw. The camera shows faintly through, so you can see where you’re going.
  2. Walk around the room. Hold the phone up and walk, pointing it at everything: walls, floor, furniture, round the back of the bed. Blocks appear wherever the phone has measured, in the room’s own colours, and stay put when you move. Anyone who wanders through gets cleared out as soon as you see past where they were. Press Done.
  3. Walk through it. You’re now in a blocky model of your room, seen from wherever you stand. Nobody who walks in is part of it, so they never appear. Show camera swaps to the live picture, with people replaced by the room behind them.

Walking needs an Android phone with Google Play Services for AR. Without it, Turn on the spot still works, from one place.

Settings

If the view looks rotated or squashed, it is Camera turned and Camera width that fix it. Hide people and How completely only apply to the camera view — among blocks nothing live is drawn at all.
