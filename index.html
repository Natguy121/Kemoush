KEMOSH

Put the phone in a Cardboard. Whatever you look at straight on stops being there.

  1. Scan. Turn slowly on the spot with nobody in front of you. The phone learns the empty room.
  2. Hunt. Phantoms are people the room doesn’t account for. A ring marks each one.
  3. Do not look at them. Hold a phantom just off to the side and its ring fills. Full ring, banished, points.
  4. Facing one unmakes it — it dissolves, the ring empties, the streak is gone. That is the whole difficulty.

One camera means both eyes see the same picture. It sits right for turning your head, but it is not true depth.

Settings

If the view looks rotated or squashed, it is Camera turned and Camera width that fix it. If nobody ever vanishes, raise Spot people and rescan.
