KEMOSH

Scan the empty room, press Done, and from then on the people in it are invisible.

  1. Scan every angle. Turn all the way round on the spot with nobody in front of you. A compass shows which directions are still dark. When it’s filled in, press Done.
  2. Now people are invisible. Anyone who walks in front of you simply isn’t drawn — you see the room behind them, wherever in view they are.

One camera means both eyes see the same picture. It sits right for turning your head, but it is not true depth.

Settings

If the view looks rotated or squashed, it is Camera turned and Camera width that fix it. If nobody ever vanishes, raise Spot people and rescan.
