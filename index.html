KEMOSH

Start in a white void. Look around, and the room builds itself back up around you out of blocks.

  1. You start in white. Nothing is known yet, so there is nothing to draw.
  2. Scan every angle. Turn all the way round on the spot with nobody in front of you. Wherever you look, the room fills in as stacked cubes — colour, shading and relief arriving together. A compass shows which directions are still blank. When it’s filled in, press Done.
  3. What you end up in is your room, rebuilt. Not the camera picture — a blocky model of it. Anyone who walks in is simply not part of that model, so they never appear.

The blocks are placed on a guessed room-shaped box, because one camera cannot measure depth. Settings → Show → the camera switches back to the plain passthrough view.

Settings

If the view looks rotated or squashed, it is Camera turned and Camera width that fix it. Hide people and How completely only apply to the camera view — among blocks nothing live is drawn at all.
